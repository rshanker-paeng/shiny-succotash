Our Story

From Startup to Scale-Up to Exit

A trusted technology partner to startups, scale-ups and private-equity-backed companies since 2012.

History

BWG Global began as xKon Consulting in Singapore and Dubai in 2012, and has operated as BWG since 2014.

The firm was started by former Convergys employees who were approached by business partners looking for their services. Early work focused on helping APAC data center service providers monetize their existing customer base. BWG then expanded into helping new businesses scale up to meet market demand, and today supports technology companies from launch through to exit.

The BWG method

Four stages, one continuous loop

Each stage hands something concrete to the next, so nothing is lost between the people who diagnose and the people who deliver.

  1. Diagnose

    Establish the facts.

    The Discovery Assessment, stakeholder interviews and a review of your code, cloud, data and delivery evidence produce a scored baseline across all four practice areas.

    You getRequirements brief and maturity scorecard

    01

  2. Design

    Decide what matters, in order.

    A target state and a roadmap sequenced by dependency and tied to business outcomes, with options and trade-offs set against your budget and deadlines.

    You getSolution design and phased roadmap

    02

  3. Deliver

    Build alongside your team.

    Former enterprise CTOs and VPs of Engineering work inside your teams, starting with quick wins that build momentum. Zero-trust security is designed in from day one.

    You getWorking change in product, platform and process

    03

  4. Measure & transfer

    Prove it, then hand it over.

    Results are reported against the original baseline. Documentation, runbooks and know-how stay with your team, so progress continues after we step back.

    You getOutcome scorecard and a team that can run it

    04

Re-baseline at each stage of growth: launch, scale, value creation, exit.
